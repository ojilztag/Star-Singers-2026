Celebrate the joy, hope and message of Christmas through music.

NAKSHATHRA GAYAKAR – STAR SINGERS 2026 is an online Christmas Carol Singing Competition presented by JKJ CREATIONS. It is a platform to celebrate the message of Christmas through music, creativity and heartfelt performance.

Whether you sing solo or as part of a group, this initiative invites participants to share the spirit of Christmas through a meaningful carol performance. Official participation categories and eligibility details will be announced by the organisers.

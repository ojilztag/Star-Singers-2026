Administration Workflow

  • Registration: Verify participant details and assign a unique Star Singers Registration ID.
  • Entry Review: Track each video submission separately from registration.
  • Evaluation: Restrict jury scoring access to authorised reviewers; Admin controls final approval.
  • Notices: Publish official updates to the public Notices page and home-page highlights.
  • Results: Review and approve winners before public release.
  • Public Profiles: Publish only organiser-approved sponsor and coordinator names, photos, designations and phone numbers.
  • Security: Keep Star Singers participant records separate from CAROLIA records.
